Move-in cleaning prepares a home for your arrival, whether you are buying or renting. Clear cabinets, drawers, closets, pantry shelves, the refrigerator and oven so our team can clean inside. We work around any furniture already in place and do not move or dispose of belongings.

Our checklist includes refrigerator, oven and microwave interiors, empty cabinets and drawers, closets, pantry shelves, kitchens, bathrooms and floors. We wipe baseboards, doors, door frames, windowsills and window frames. Interior window glass and tracks, wet-wiping blinds, dishwasher interiors, sliding-door glass and tracks, and garage sweeping are paid add-ons.

If drywall or construction dust remains after a new build or remodel, book post-construction cleaning so the quote covers that work. Electricity and running water must be on for either service.

Share your closing date or lease start and when you expect to have access. We coordinate the cleaning before your belongings arrive.

Is This Right for You?

Who it’s for

Anyone moving into a new home, whether purchased or rented — buyers coordinating around a closing date, renters starting a lease, and realtors and property managers preparing homes for new buyers and tenants.

  • You have keys and want the home cleaned before unpacking
  • Cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator and pantry are cleared so our team can clean inside them
  • Partial furniture move-in is fine — our team works around it
  • You're moving into a rental and want kitchens, bathrooms, floors and empty storage spaces cleaned before your boxes arrive
  • You're coordinating around a closing date or lease start and need a team that can slot in between key handoff and moving day

Scope & Preparation

A few important notes

Scope

Cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator and pantry should be empty so our team can clean inside them. Partial furniture move-in is fine — we work around it.

Items left in the home

We don't move, store, donate, or discard items left in the home. Cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator and pantry should be cleared by you so our team can clean inside them.

Utilities

Electricity and running water must be on at the time of service.

Construction dust

Move-in cleaning covers any home free of construction dust — new build, remodel, resale, or rental; homes still carrying drywall or construction dust are quoted as a post-construction clean instead.

What is included in pricing

Move-in cleaning pricing covers the interior of one oven and one refrigerator. Additional ovens or refrigerators are quoted separately ($60 per oven, $40 per fridge). Interior dishwasher cleaning is available as an add-on ($40).

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a move-in cleaning different from a deep clean?

Move-in cleaning adds empty cabinet, drawer, pantry and closet interiors, plus refrigerator and oven interiors, to the deep-clean scope. Microwave interior cleaning is included in both services. Interior window glass and tracks, wet-wiping blinds, sliding-door glass and tracks, and dishwasher interiors are paid add-ons. Clear storage spaces and appliances before the visit; we work around any furniture already in place.

When should I schedule the move-in cleaning?

After you have keys and before any furniture arrives. That window is the sweet spot. We can work around your closing date — tell us when you expect to take possession and we'll hold a time block. Once you confirm keys, we confirm the appointment.

Do you clean new construction homes?

Move-in cleaning is for homes free of construction dust, including new builds and remodels after construction cleanup. If drywall or construction dust remains, choose post-construction cleaning so the quote covers that work.

What if the home isn't fully empty when you arrive?

Move-in cleans are flexible — partial furniture move-in is fine. Cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator and pantry should be empty so we can clean inside them. We work around items that are already in place.

Do you move or remove items left in the home?

No. We don't move, store, donate, or discard items left in the home. Cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator and pantry should be cleared by you so our team can clean inside them.

Do utilities need to be on for a move-in clean?

Yes. Electricity and running water must be on at the time of service.
