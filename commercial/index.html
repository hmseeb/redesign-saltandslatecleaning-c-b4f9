The Salt & Slate Difference

Built for Commercial Standards

Licensed & Insured

Every Salt & Slate crew carries general liability and property damage coverage. We carry the insurance your facility management requires, and we provide certificates of insurance on request.

Industry-Aware Cleaning

We agree on your facility’s cleaning checklist before service starts. Share the surfaces, access rules and product requirements for your workplace so the quote reflects the work your team needs.

Flexible Scheduling

After-hours, early morning, weekends, or around active operations. We build a cleaning schedule that fits your business rhythm, not the other way around. Daily, weekly, or custom frequencies available.

Background-Checked Crews

Every person we send to your facility has passed a background check. For facilities requiring badge access, secure entry, or confidentiality protocols, we coordinate directly with your security team.

Consistent Point of Contact

You get a dedicated account contact who knows your facility, your preferences, and your standards. No call centers, no ticket queues. Direct communication with someone who knows your space.

Commercial-Grade Products

Commercial-grade disinfectants, HEPA-filtered vacuums, and professional equipment matched to your surfaces and industry. Fragrance-free and low-VOC product options available on request for sensitive environments.
