Frequently Asked Questions

What areas in Nevada do you serve?

We serve Henderson, Las Vegas, Summerlin, North Las Vegas, and Boulder City in the Las Vegas Valley.

Do you provide cleaning for short-term rentals in Las Vegas?

Yes. Airbnb and short-term rental cleaning is a significant part of our Las Vegas operation. We sync with your booking calendar for same-day turnovers with Superhost-standard checklists.

How does Las Vegas hard water affect cleaning?

Las Vegas has some of the hardest municipal water in the country. Calcium and mineral buildup on glass shower doors, faucets, and tile is aggressive. We use specialized hard water removal products and recommend increased frequency for glass and fixture cleaning.
